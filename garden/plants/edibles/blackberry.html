PLANT JOURNAL

Blackberry

Rubus fruticosus

Type

Perennial fruiting bramble

Bloom

Late spring to early summer

Light

Full sun

Water

Moderate; consistent moisture while fruiting

Garden Notes

Blackberry growing in the Root & Brass garden. This page will become a record of bloom times, seasonal changes, photographs, and observations from year to year.

Seasonal Record

2026 Heavily Neglected this year! And looking to be my most productive year yet. I did not prune, weed, and only watered with intent to keep alive this year.

2025 Pruned early this year with the Raspberries. Come August I only received one handfull of Blackberries. I weeded and watered with a drip liner.

2024 Planted this year, purchased from Homedepot in store. The bush shot off like a rocket with lots of growth and a good fair amount of berries.
