Garden Notes

A Strawberry growing in the Root & Brass garden. This page will become a record of bloom times, seasonal changes, photographs, and observations from year to year.

Seasonal Record

2026 I don't remember exactly but I'm most certain I got my strawberries many years ago from my grandpa's garden. This year I nearly killed them all. I had them in a very neglected wooden planter box, knowing they did better in the ground previous years. July when they were nearly done for I moved them out to the southwest garden where they surely would risk being eaten, but they would get water. in August I'm already seening new life. They should be able to carry on for a long time now.
