A papery sheath called an ocrea surrounds the stem at each leaf node and helps distinguish Persicaria species. Modern human clinical evidence for Persicaria maculosa is limited, so these uses are recorded as historical and traditional rather than proven medical treatments. culinary_uses: |- Young leaves of positively identified Lady's Thumb have been eaten as a cooked or raw wild green in some traditions.
