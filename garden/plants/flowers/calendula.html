PLANT JOURNAL

Calendula

Calendula officinalis

Type

Cool-season annual

Bloom

Late spring until frost

Light

Full sun to partial shade

Water

Moderate; water when the top inch is dry

Garden Notes

A Calendula growing in the Root & Brass garden. This page will become a record of bloom times, seasonal changes, photographs, and observations from year to year.

Seasonal Record

2026 Seeds were given to me last fall, I planted them this spring new the house. Some snow in summer kept coering them and they weren't getting the sun the deserved. So I took a change in the middle of July and dug them up and plopped them in the sun. The looked sickly for about 2 weeks before they exploded.
