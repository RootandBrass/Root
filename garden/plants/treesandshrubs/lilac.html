PLANT JOURNAL

Lilac

Syringa vulgaris

Type

Deciduous flowering shrub

Bloom

Mid- to late spring

Light

Full sun

Water

Low to moderate; deep watering during dry periods

Garden Notes

A Lilac growing in the Root & Brass garden. This page will become a record of bloom times, seasonal changes, photographs, and observations from year to year.

Seasonal Record

2026 This spring we pulled a second large lilac from the same location split it into 3 and placed them in an emply dirt spot on the east along Pine Ave. I then purched 12 very small yellow lilac and placed groups of them in between all o f the replanted bushes to give it some interest later in their years.

2025 Fall of this year, we pulled one of the 3 giant lilac pushed that were here when we moved in. We split it into 3 and planted then next to our Garage adjacent from the neightbor garage they lived under for who knows how long.
