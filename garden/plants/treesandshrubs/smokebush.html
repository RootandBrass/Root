PLANT JOURNAL

Smoke Bush

Cotinus coggygria

Type

Deciduous flowering shrub

Bloom

Late spring to early summer; plumes persist

Light

Full sun

Water

Low to moderate; drought-tolerant once established

Garden Notes

Aspens growing in the Root & Brass garden. This page will become a record of bloom times, seasonal changes, photographs, and observations from year to year.

Seasonal Record

2026 This Adorable bush I got from my mother alond with all the Cheyennes. I would say he is my buddy. Loves him a little sun but not too much. And when he is happy he looks a little fussy. Never seen anything like him and I'm happy he doesn't mind where I set his roots.
