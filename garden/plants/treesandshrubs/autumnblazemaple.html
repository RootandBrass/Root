Seasonal Record

2026 I am at war with the dear. They have nibbled their last leaf! I have 1 Maple Tree and now what is left of 2 I now call bushes. I honestly haven't tried hard enough to protect them and admittedly shocked everyyear when they are nibbled or rubbed to the nub. But I will one day have 3 tall standing beautiful Maple trees.
