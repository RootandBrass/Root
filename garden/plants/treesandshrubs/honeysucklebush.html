PLANT JOURNAL

Honeysuckle Bush

Lonicera spp.

Type

Deciduous flowering shrub

Bloom

Late spring to early summer

Light

Full sun to partial shade

Water

Low to moderate; drought-tolerant once established

Garden Notes

a honeysuckle Bush growing in the Root & Brass garden. This page will become a record of bloom times, seasonal changes, photographs, and observations from year to year.

Seasonal Record

2026 I have a few of these that were presant and thriving when I moved in. They can almost look beautiful all summer with almost no extra love or water. If I were to care for them I'm sure they would be emaculate. We one a row on the west driveway and one on each side of the entrance of the alley. The one on the east we litterally plopped there. It surevived and has been doing it thing since.
