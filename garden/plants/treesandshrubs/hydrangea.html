PLANT JOURNAL

Hydrangea

Hydrangea macrophylla and Hydrangea paniculata

Type

Deciduous flowering shrub

Bloom

Midsummer to autumn

Light

Morning sun with afternoon shade; panicle types tolerate more sun

Water

Moderate to high; keep consistently moist

Garden Notes

A Hydrangea growing in the Root & Brass garden. This page will become a record of bloom times, seasonal changes, photographs, and observations from year to year.

Seasonal Record
