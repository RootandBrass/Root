Garden Journal

August 2026

The Garden in August

August is always the hardest. The water cannot keep up; everything is dry and windblown. At this point, I am watering simply to keep things alive and focusing on the planters and the plants that need strength going into winter. Two weeks ago, after noticing several weeks of new growth on the hydrangeas in my tall black planters, I deadheaded the old flowers. The new growth leapt forward and is beginning to produce another round of blooms. I am excited by the response, although I do not expect the same result every year.

July 2026

What is Growing in July

What is growing, you ask? Everything! I harvested honeysuckle, my calendula has a bloom, marigolds grown from seed are beginning to flower, and the Cheyenne privets were full earlier this month. Surprisingly, almost everything is still doing well and the lawn remains green. I changed my watering routine this year to a deep watering about once a week. The deer did browse a few things this month, including a hosta and my middle maple tree. I need to protect those trees soon.
