Garden Journal

August 2026

The Garden in August

August is always the hardest. Water can't keep up. Everything is dry and windy and dieing. And I'm watering at this point just to keep it alive and I fucus more on my planters and the things that need the strength come winter. My tall black planters that have hydrangeas this year, I chose to dead head the flowers two weeks back after several weeks of noticing new growth. After dead heading the old flower the new growth leaped and is starting to produce a new round of flowers. I'm really excited about this but I do not expect the same response in future years.

July 2026

What is Growing in July

What is growing, you ask? Everything!!! I harvested honeysucle, my colenjula has a bloom, merigolds from seed are starting to bloom, the cheyenne bushes were in full earlier this month. Surprisingly most everything is still doing well. The lawn is still green. I changed up my watering this year to a deep water. I basically water once a week. The deer did munch on a few things this month. A hosta and my middle maple tree. I need to get these trees protected and soon.
