---
title: Pizza King Breadsicks
category: copycat-recipes
---
1 can refrigerated pizza dough, 

2Tbsp butter, 

1 tsp dill weed, 

1/4 tsp garlic powder. 

Roll out pizza dough on sprayed cookie sheet, cut into 8-10 sticks, bake 425 for 8 minutes. In bowl melt butter, dill, and garlic together. Baste breadsticks after 8 min of cooking and cook 2 more minutes. Serve with cheese sauce. Yummy!
