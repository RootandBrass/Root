---
title: "Cobbler Crumble Topping"
category: desserts
---

## Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup packed dark brown sugar
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- Pinch salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon or 1/8 teaspoon ground or grated nutmeg (optional)
- Up to 8 tablespoons unsalted butter, slightly softened and cut into pieces

## Preparation

1. Combine the flour, both sugars, salt, and cinnamon or nutmeg, if using, in a medium bowl. Rub in-the butter with your fingertips until it’s well blended and the mixture crumbles coarsely; it should hold together when you pinch it. Refrigerate until needed.

*Notebook note: 8 tablespoons of butter seemed like too much, so begin with less and add only enough for coarse crumbs to form.*
