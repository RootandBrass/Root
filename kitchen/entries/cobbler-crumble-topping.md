category: desserts

## Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup packed dark brown sugar
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- Pinch salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon or 1/8 teaspoon ground or grated nutmeg (optional)
- 8 tablespoons slightly softened unsalted butter, cut-into pieces (TOO much butter)

## Preparation

1. Combine the flour, both sugars, salt, and cinnamon or nutmeg, if using, in a medium bowl. Rub in-the butter with your fingertips until it’s well blended and the mixture crumbles coarsely; it should hold together when you pinch it. Refrigerate until needed.
2. Oatmeal topping variation
3. Adding oatmeal makes a more voluminous topping with a rustic, crumbly texture. Add
4. cup old- fashioned oats
5. to the master recipe.
6. Cornmeal topping variation
7. Cornmeal adds some unexpected crunch but makes the topping a bit less crumbly. Add
8. /4 cup cornmeal
9. to the master recipe.
10. Nut topping variation
11. After rubbing in the butter, add
12. /3 cup chopped walnuts, pecans, or hazelnuts, or sliced almonds
13. to any of the recipes above.
