category: drinks

Chocolate cake:

Halfway fill a shot glass with vodka and halfway with Frangelico.

Cut a lemon wedge and pour sugar on a plate then coat the lemon wedge with sugar.

Nice healthy coating of sugar.

Drink then bite.

It should taste like chocolate cake at the end.

And that's why we call it chocolate cake! ;)

Tip: it's always better to have slightly more frangelico than it is to have vodka.

Amaretto sour:

Pour about four shots of amaretto in a glass with ice then top it off with squirt. Nice and

easy.

Tip: if you want the drink stronger add vodka.

Boilermaker:

Pour entire contents of the 12 ounce beer into a glass large enough to hold that and a

shot of Crown. Drop the crown in and chug all the way. Not so flavorful but it really

packs a Wallop.

Tequila sunrise:

2 to 4 shots of tequila in a glass with ice topped off with orange juice, add about a shot

of Grenadine.

Tip: I like to stir the drink before I add the Grenadine. The Grenadine will sink to the

bottom and gives it a really neat theatrical sunrise look to it. However, the drink taste

better if you stir the entire drink before drinking.
