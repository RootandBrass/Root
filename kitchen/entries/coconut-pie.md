---
title: "Coconut Pie"
category: desserts
---

## Ingredients

- 2 cups milk
- 1 cup shredded coconut
- 4 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- 8 tablespoons butter
- ¾ cup sugar
- ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a pie plate.
2. Blend the milk, coconut, eggs, vanilla, flour, butter, and sugar until well combined.
3. Pour into the prepared pie plate and sprinkle nutmeg over the top.
4. Bake for 45 minutes.
