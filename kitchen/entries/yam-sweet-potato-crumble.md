- 4 cups mashed [sweet potatoes](https://www.food.com/about/sweet-potato-307) 
- 1⁄4 cup  [sugar](https://www.food.com/about/sugar-139) 
- 1 tsp  [vanilla](https://www.food.com/about/vanilla-350) 
- 1⁄3 cup [half-and-half](https://www.food.com/about/half-and-half-445) 
- 2  [eggs](https://www.food.com/about/egg-142), beaten  
- Pinch  [salt](https://www.food.com/about/salt-359) 
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon  
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg 
- 1/8 tsp ginger 
- 1 Tbsp brown sugar



  

Bake 350 for 45 min 

Add marshmallows and broil until brown.
