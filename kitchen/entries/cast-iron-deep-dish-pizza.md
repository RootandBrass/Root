## Dough

- 3 ¼ cups (16.25 ounces) all-purpose flour
- ½ cup yellow cornmeal
- 1 ½ teaspoons salt
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2 ¼ teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 ¼ cups warm water (10 ounces)
- 7 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided (3 tablespoons melted, 4 tablespoons softened)
- 1 teaspoon olive oil for the bowl

## Sauce

- 1 (15-ounce) can tomato sauce
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon dried parsley
- ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes

## Pizza Fixings

- ¾ pound low-moisture mozzarella, sliced
- ½ pound sweet Italian sausage
- ½ red pepper, thinly sliced
- ½ green pepper, thinly sliced
- ¼ sweet onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cup asiago cheese, grated
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

### Dough

1. Add all dry ingredients to a bowl and mix well. Slowly add the warm water and melted butter until the dough ball comes together.
2. Knead for 8–10 minutes until glossy and smooth. Alternatively, use a mixer with a dough hook on medium for 5–6 minutes.
3. Let the dough rise in a lightly oiled bowl for about 1 hour, or until doubled in size.
4. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and roll it into roughly a 16 × 12-inch rectangle.
5. Spread the softened butter over the dough, leaving about ½ inch around the edges.
6. Roll the dough up like a cigar, then fold it over itself into thirds. Pinch the seams around the dough to form a ball.
7. Let the dough rise a second time in the refrigerator for 1 hour.

### Sauce

Stir all sauce ingredients together in a medium bowl and set aside. Adjust seasonings to taste.

### Pizza

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Roll the dough into about a 15-inch circle.
3. Fit the dough into a 12-inch skillet, working it up the sides to form a crust and pressing it into the corners. Crimp around the edges.
4. Add the sliced mozzarella to the bottom, followed by the sauce. Use all of the sauce for a very saucy pizza, or ½–⅔ for less sauce.
5. Add small chunks of uncooked sweet Italian sausage, sliced peppers, sliced onion, and grated asiago cheese.
6. Season with salt and pepper.
7. Bake at 375°F for 55–60 minutes.
8. Let the pizza rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
