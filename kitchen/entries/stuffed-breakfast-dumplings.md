Basic Dough (Makes 6–8 large breakfast dumplings)

2 cups all-purpose flour

2 teaspoons baking powder

1 teaspoon salt

1 teaspoon garlic powder

1 teaspoon onion powder

1¼ cups plain Greek yogurt (full-fat or 2% works best)

## Instructions

1. Whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, garlic powder, and onion powder.
2. Stir in the Greek yogurt until a shaggy dough forms.
3. Knead for 2–3 minutes until smooth. If it’s sticky, add flour 1 tablespoon at a time. If it’s dry, add yogurt 1 teaspoon at a time.
4. Let the dough rest for 10–15 minutes before rolling.
5. Roll each portion into a circle about ⅛–¼ inch thick, add your filling, and pinch tightly to seal.
