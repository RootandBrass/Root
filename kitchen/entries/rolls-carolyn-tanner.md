---
title: Rolls Carolyn Tanner
category: bread
---
- 1 cup Milk
- 2 Tb Sugar
- 2 Tb Butter 
- 3/4 t Salt
- 1 cake yeast
- 3-3 1/2 cups flour

Make into salt dough kneading 2 min. Let rise until double in bulk. Roll to 1/2 inch thickness. Cut and fold and brush with butter.

Let rise until double in bulk and bake
