category: copycat-recipes

Ingredients 

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise (l use light mayonnaise) 
- 1/4 cup honey 
- 2 tablespoons yellow mustard 
- 2 tablespoons ketchup 
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice 
- 1 teaspoon brown sugar 
- 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar 

Instructions 

1. Combine all the ingredients in a small bowl and whisk to combine. 

2. Store in the refrigerator in an airtight container for up to 4 weeks. 

Note: Always use your best judgement. If it smells or looks off from when you first made it, toss it.
