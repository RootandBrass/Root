These are Grandma Layton's Crepes who spent time in France for several years

- 3 cups milk
- 3 eggs
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp baking power

Butter makes the Crepe not stick to the pan and keeps it from sticking. I usually start with a whole tablespoon of butter in the hot skillet and poor a small amount of batter over the butter. Moved the batter around until it is an even coating. Flip when edges start to crisp. I gradually add less butter before each crepe because of the amount left in the pan each time.

The amount of batter will depend on desired thickness of crepe.
