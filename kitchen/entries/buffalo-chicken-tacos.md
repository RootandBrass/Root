title: "Buffalo Chicken Tacos"
category: meals-chicken

## Ingredients

- 1 pound chicken breast
- ½ teaspoon each salt and pepper
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 packet ranch seasoning
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 1 onion, chopped
- ½ cup buffalo sauce, plus more to taste
- Tortillas
- Shredded cheese
- Butter for the skillet

## Instructions

1. Add the chicken, salt, pepper, paprika, ranch seasoning, garlic, onion, and buffalo sauce to a slow cooker. Stir together.
2. Cook on low for about 4 hours, then shred the chicken. Add more buffalo sauce if desired.
3. Melt a little butter in a skillet. Add a tortilla, shredded cheese, and chicken, then fold the tortilla over.
4. Cook on both sides until golden brown.
