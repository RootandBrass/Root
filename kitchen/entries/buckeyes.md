---
title: "Buckeyes"
category: desserts
---

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups peanut butter
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 6 cups confectioners' sugar
- 4 cups semisweet chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Mix the peanut butter, butter, vanilla, and confectioners' sugar. The dough will look dry. Roll into 1-inch balls on a waxed-paper-lined baking sheet.
2. Press a toothpick into each ball and freeze until firm, about 30 minutes.
3. Melt the chocolate chips in a double boiler, stirring frequently until smooth.
4. Holding the toothpick, dip each ball in chocolate, leaving a small circle of peanut butter visible at the top.
5. Return to the baking sheet and refrigerate until ready to serve.

*Notebook note: the original version was very sweet.*
