---
title: Gma Maroney's Mac & Cheese
category: casseroles
---
2 cups cooked macaroni  

1 can evaporated milk 

3 eggs 

1/2 cup butter 

Pinch of salt 

Velveta cheese 

  

Mix hot noodles with butter until melted. Layer 

 in pan noodles and slices of cheese. Mix milk, eggs and salt pour over noodles and cheese. Cover with foil. Bake at 300 to 350 for about 30  

to 45 min. I kind of mix mine while it’s cooking so it cooks more evenly. Remove foil and cover with regular cheese if you’d like cook 15 min more.
