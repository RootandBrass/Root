---
title: "Jimmy Deans Hot Sausage Seasoning"
category: "Seasonings"
---

Sea salt: 1 tsp
Cayenne pepper: 1/2 tsp (adjust for more heat)
Rubbed sage: 1/4 tsp
Ground black pepper: 1/4 tsp
Red pepper flakes: 1/4 tsp
Coriander: 1/4 tsp
MSG (such as Accent): 1/4 tsp (optional)
Instructions
Combine the spices: Place all the herbs and spices in a small bowl and mix them together.
Mix with meat: Add the ground pork to a large mixing bowl. Sprinkle the seasoning mix over the meat.
Blend thoroughly: Use your hands to thoroughly work the spices into the ground pork until the seasoning is evenly distributed.
Rest the meat: Cover the sausage and place it in the refrigerator for a day or two to let the flavors fully develop and marry before cooking.
Cook: Form the mixture into patties and fry in a skillet over medium heat until browned and cooked through, or crumble and cook in a pan.
