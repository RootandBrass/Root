ABOUT ROOT & BRASS

About

Root & Brass is a personal collection of old-house history, garden notes, projects, discoveries, and the things that gather meaning when you live with them long enough.

The Place

At the center of Root & Brass is 301 Ruby, a 1910 home with more than a century of history still tucked into its walls, details, and surrounding ground.

The Garden

The garden is an ongoing experiment in growing, learning the seasons, keeping what thrives, and trying again when nature has other plans.

The Journal

The journal is where the pieces come together: stories, research, projects, observations, discoveries, and whatever seems worth keeping.
