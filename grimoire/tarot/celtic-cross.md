# Celtic Cross

A ten-card spread for looking at a situation in depth.

1. **Present** — the heart of the situation.
2. **The Problem** — the challenge, crossing influence, or immediate obstacle.
3. **Past** — what has shaped the present.
4. **Future** — what is developing next.
5. **Your Strength** — resources, perspective, or qualities available to you.
6. **Root of the Problem** — the underlying cause or foundation.
7. **How to Face It** — your approach, attitude, or position.
8. **External Influences** — people, circumstances, and forces around you.
9. **Advice** — guidance, hopes/fears, or what deserves attention.
10. **Outcome** — the likely direction if the current pattern continues.

## Layout

Card 1 sits at the center, crossed by Card 2. Card 3 is to the left, Card 4 to the right, Card 5 above, and Card 6 below. Cards 7 through 10 form a vertical column on the right, rising from 7 at the bottom to 10 at the top.
