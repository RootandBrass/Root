geology: |+
  - An opaque red variety of **chalcedony**, the microcrystalline form of quartz
  - Its red, rust, and brown coloring comes primarily from iron-oxide inclusions
  - Often displays mottling, veins, spots, or bands in shades of red, brown, cream, and gray
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **6.5 to 7**
  - Historically used for carvings, seals, tools, jewelry, and decorative objects

care: |+
