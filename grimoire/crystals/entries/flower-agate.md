geology: |+
  - A variety of **chalcedony**, the microcrystalline form of quartz
  - Known for opaque plume-like inclusions that resemble blossoms, seeds, or flowering branches
  - Commonly found in soft pink, peach, cream, white, and translucent tones
  - Material marketed as Flower Agate is strongly associated with deposits from Madagascar
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **6.5 to 7**
  - Each pattern is naturally distinct, with some pieces showing druzy pockets or small cavities

associations: |+
  - Traditionally associated with **growth, potential, new beginnings, and gentle transformation**
  - Connected with the **heart and root chakras**
  - Its flower-like inclusions symbolize ideas and intentions coming into bloom
  - Often used to encourage patience, courage, and steady progress
  - Associated with nurturing dreams while remaining grounded in practical action

uses: |+
  - Encourages personal growth and new beginnings
  - Supports turning intentions into steady, practical action
  - Helps build confidence during periods of change
  - Traditionally used to release self-doubt and fear of failure
  - Encourages patience with slow or uneven progress
  - Useful for manifestation, goal-setting, and creative work
  - Supports emotional balance while pursuing long-term plans
  - Lovely for garden magic, spring altars, and rituals of renewal
  - Can be kept near a journal, planner, or vision board as a symbol of unfolding potential
  - Encourages appreciation for small signs of progress

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft, damp cloth**
  - Briefly rinse with cool or lukewarm water, then dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid prolonged soaking, especially if the stone has druzy pockets or fractures
  - Avoid saltwater, harsh chemicals, steam, and sudden temperature changes
  - Protect druzy areas and natural cavities from knocks and trapped moisture
  - Store separately from harder stones that could scratch its polish
  - Limit prolonged strong sunlight to help preserve its soft coloring

pairings: Moss Agate, Rose Quartz, Carnelian, Citrine, Clear Quartz
