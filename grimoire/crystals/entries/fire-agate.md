geology: |+
  - A rare variety of **chalcedony** containing thin layers of iron oxides, commonly limonite or goethite
  - Light interacting with these layered inclusions creates flashes of red, orange, gold, green, and occasionally violet
  - Its iridescent effect is sometimes described as fire, flame, or peacock coloring
  - Commonly forms in botryoidal or grape-like layers that are carefully polished to reveal the fire
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **6.5 to 7**
  - Important deposits are associated with volcanic regions of Mexico and the southwestern United States

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft, damp cloth**
  - Briefly rinse with cool or lukewarm water, then dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **smoke, sound, moonlight, or selenite**
  - Avoid prolonged soaking, saltwater, harsh chemicals, and abrasive cleaners
  - Avoid steam, ultrasonic cleaners, and sudden temperature changes
  - Protect carved or polished fire-bearing layers from hard impacts
  - Store separately from harder stones that could scratch its surface
  - Limit prolonged strong sunlight and excessive heat
