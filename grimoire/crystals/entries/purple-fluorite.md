geology: |+
  - A purple variety of **fluorite**, composed of calcium fluoride
  - Commonly forms cubic or octahedral crystals and may show bands of violet, lavender, clear, green, or blue
  - Its color is related to structural defects, trace elements, and natural irradiation within the crystal
  - Has a Mohs hardness of **4**, making it relatively soft
  - Possesses perfect cleavage in four directions and can chip or split easily
  - Some specimens fluoresce under ultraviolet light

care: |+
  - Clean with a **soft, dry or barely damp cloth**
