geology: |+
  - A copper carbonate hydroxide mineral known for vivid green bands and swirling patterns
  - Forms as botryoidal masses, crusts, fibrous aggregates, and occasionally small crystals
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **3.5 to 4**
  - Often develops in the oxidized zones of copper deposits
  - Its dust contains copper and should not be inhaled or ingested
  - Some imitation material is made from resin or reconstituted fragments with unnaturally uniform banding

care: |+
