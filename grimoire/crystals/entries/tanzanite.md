geology: |+
  - The blue-to-violet gem variety of the mineral **zoisite**
  - Commercially significant material comes from a small mining region in northern Tanzania
  - Displays pleochroism, showing different blue, violet, and sometimes burgundy tones from different directions
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **6 to 7**
  - Possesses cleavage and is vulnerable to chipping, thermal shock, and sudden impact
  - Most gem-quality Tanzanite is gently heat-treated to emphasize its blue and violet colors

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft cloth and mild soapy water**
