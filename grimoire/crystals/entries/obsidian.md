title: Obsidian
variety: Obsidian
color: Black
element: Earth & Fire
chakra: Root
zodiac: Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn
geology: |+
  - A natural **volcanic glass** formed when silica-rich lava cools too quickly for crystals to develop
  - Usually black, though inclusions and formation patterns can create snowflake, mahogany, rainbow, or golden-sheen varieties
  - Breaks with sharp, curved **conchoidal fractures**
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **5 to 5.5**
  - Historically shaped into cutting tools, arrowheads, mirrors, and ornamental objects

associations: |+
  - Traditionally associated with **protection, grounding, truth, and energetic boundaries**
  - Connected with the **root chakra**
  - Often used to reveal hidden patterns and encourage honest self-reflection
  - Associated with releasing unwanted attachments and clearing stagnant energy
  - Sometimes regarded as a spiritual shield during ritual or divination

uses: |+
  - Supports grounding and steadiness during stressful periods
  - Traditionally used for **protection and energetic shielding**
  - Encourages honesty, self-awareness, and recognition of difficult patterns
  - Useful for boundary-setting and release work
  - Can anchor meditation and shadow-work practices
  - Often placed near entrances or workspaces as a protective symbol
  - Helpful in rituals for banishing, cleansing, and severing unwanted ties
  - Can be carried when courage and clear judgment are needed

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft, damp cloth**
  - Briefly rinse with cool or lukewarm water, then dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **smoke, sound, moonlight, or selenite**
  - Avoid harsh chemicals and extreme temperature changes
  - Protect from falls and hard impacts because obsidian can chip or shatter
  - Handle broken pieces carefully; fractured edges can be extremely sharp
  - Store separately from harder stones that may scratch its polished surface

pairings: Smoky Quartz, Black Tourmaline, Hematite, Clear Quartz, Labradorite
