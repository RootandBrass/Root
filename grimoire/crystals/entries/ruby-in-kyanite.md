geology: |+
  - A naturally occurring combination of **ruby (red corundum)** and **kyanite (an aluminum silicate)** in the same rock
  - Ruby appears as red to pinkish-red crystals or patches within a blue to blue-gray kyanite-bearing matrix
  - Kyanite has direction-dependent hardness, approximately **4.5–5 parallel** to its length and **6.5–7 across** it; ruby has a Mohs hardness of **9**
  - Some specimens also include mica or other associated minerals
  - The contrasting components make the material suitable for display, cabochons, and carefully handled polished pieces
  - The trade name **Ruby in Kyanite** describes a mineral association, not a single mineral species

associations: |+
  - In modern crystal folklore, ruby is associated with **courage, vitality, passion, and protection**
  - Kyanite is commonly associated with **clarity, honest communication, intuition, and calm**
  - Together, they symbolically unite fiery resolve with thoughtful expression
  - Often linked with the **root, heart, throat, and third-eye chakras**
  - These are spiritual traditions and symbolic associations, not scientifically established effects

uses: |+
  - Place on an altar for courage balanced with discernment
  - Hold during reflection on speaking honestly and acting with compassion
  - Use symbolically in intentions concerning confidence, boundaries, and communication
  - Incorporate into meditation or journaling about purpose and self-expression
  - Keep as a reminder to pair bold action with a clear mind
  - Suitable for personal rituals centered on motivation, resilience, and inner balance
  - Enjoy as a naturally contrasting mineral specimen

care: |+
  - Dust with a **soft dry cloth** or gently wipe with a barely damp cloth; dry promptly
  - Avoid long soaking, saltwater, acids, household cleaners, steam, and ultrasonic cleaning
  - Kyanite can split or chip along its cleavage planes, even though ruby itself is very hard
  - Avoid drops, strong blows, and abrasive scrubbing
  - Store separately from harder or rougher stones to reduce surface damage
  - For symbolic cleansing, choose **moonlight, sound, or a smoke-free ritual** rather than water or salt
  - Do not make gem elixirs or ingest mineral powders; the identity and treatments of a specimen may be uncertain

pairings: Rose Quartz, Clear Quartz, Sodalite, Red Jasper, Amethyst
