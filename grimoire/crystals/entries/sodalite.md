geology: |+
  - A sodium-rich feldspathoid mineral commonly found in blue massive form
  - Often contains white calcite veins or patches
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **5.5 to 6**
  - Usually opaque, although rare material may be translucent
  - A principal blue component of the rock lapis lazuli can be sodalite-group material, though ordinary Sodalite usually lacks visible pyrite
  - Some specimens fluoresce under ultraviolet light

associations: |+
  - Traditionally associated with **logic, truth, communication, self-knowledge, and mental order**
  - Connected with the **throat and third-eye chakras**
  - Often used to balance intuition with rational thought
  - Associated with honest expression and thoughtful speech
  - Sometimes called a stone of insight and disciplined thinking

uses: |+
  - Encourages clear, logical, and honest communication

  - Can be kept near a desk or carried during difficult conversations
  - Supports recognizing patterns and questioning assumptions

care: |+
  - Clean with a **soft, dry or barely damp cloth**
  - If rinsed, use cool water briefly and dry immediately
  - Avoid prolonged soaking, saltwater, acids, and harsh chemicals
  - Do not use steam or ultrasonic cleaners
  - Cleanse energetically with **smoke, sound, moonlight, or selenite**
  - Limit prolonged strong sunlight to help preserve its color
  - Protect from scratches, knocks, and sudden temperature changes
  - Store separately from harder stones
