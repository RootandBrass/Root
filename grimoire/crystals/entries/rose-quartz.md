geology: |+
  - A pink variety of **quartz**, composed primarily of silicon dioxide
  - Its color is generally linked to microscopic mineral inclusions within the stone
  - Usually translucent to cloudy, with shades ranging from very pale blush to deeper rose pink
  - Has a Mohs hardness of **7**, making it fairly durable but still vulnerable to chips and fractures
  - Natural pieces may contain veils, inclusions, or internal fractures that give the stone a soft, misty appearance

associations: |+
  - Traditionally associated with **love, compassion, tenderness, and emotional healing**
  - Connected with the **heart chakra**
  - Often used to encourage self-love, forgiveness, and gentle communication
  - Associated with harmony in relationships, family bonds, and peaceful homes
  - Sometimes called a stone of unconditional love

uses: |+
  - Encourages **self-love and self-acceptance**
  - Supports compassion, patience, and emotional gentleness
  - Traditionally used for soothing grief, heartache, and emotional tension
  - Encourages forgiveness and the release of resentment
  - Supports loving communication and harmony in relationships
  - Useful in rituals for love, friendship, family, and reconciliation
  - Creates a soft, peaceful atmosphere in bedrooms and shared spaces
  - Can be carried as a reminder to respond with kindness
  - Lovely for heart-centered meditation, altars, and intention work
  - Often placed near the bed or in the home to encourage comfort and calm

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft, damp cloth**
  - Briefly rinse with cool or lukewarm water if needed, then dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid prolonged soaking, especially if the stone has visible fractures
  - Avoid saltwater and harsh household chemicals
  - Avoid steam and ultrasonic cleaners when the stone is fractured or included
  - Keep out of prolonged, strong sunlight because its pink color may fade over time
  - Store separately from harder stones or sharp objects that could damage its polished surface
