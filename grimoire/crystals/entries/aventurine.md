title: Aventurine
variety: Green Aventurine
color: Green
element: Earth
chakra: Heart
zodiac: Aries, Taurus, Virgo
geology: |+
  - A variety of **quartz** containing small mineral inclusions that create a subtle glittering effect called aventurescence
  - Green Aventurine usually receives its color and sparkle from inclusions of fuchsite, a chromium-rich mica
  - Color ranges from pale mint and soft sage to deeper forest green
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **6.5 to 7**
  - Commonly found in massive form and shaped into tumbled stones, carvings, beads, and cabochons

associations: |+
  - Traditionally associated with **luck, opportunity, growth, prosperity, and optimism**
  - Connected with the **heart chakra**
  - Often called a stone of opportunity or good fortune
  - Associated with confidence, renewal, and the willingness to try something new
  - Frequently used in abundance, garden, and personal-growth practices

uses: |+
  - Traditionally carried for luck and new opportunities
  - Encourages optimism, confidence, and forward movement
  - Supports prosperity work grounded in practical choices
  - Useful for goal-setting, business, money, and career intentions
  - Encourages emotional balance and recovery after disappointment
  - Supports growth in gardens, creative projects, and personal habits
  - Can be kept near a planner, wallet, workspace, or houseplant
  - Lovely for spring rituals, garden magic, and new beginnings
  - Encourages taking reasonable chances while remaining grounded
  - Supports harmony between the heart and practical decision-making

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft, damp cloth**
  - Briefly rinse with cool or lukewarm water, then dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid prolonged soaking, saltwater, and harsh household chemicals
  - Avoid steam, ultrasonic cleaners, and sudden temperature changes
  - Protect from hard impacts and natural fracture lines
  - Store separately from harder stones that could scratch its polish
  - Limit prolonged strong sunlight to help preserve its color

pairings: Citrine, Jade, Moss Agate, Clear Quartz, Rose Quartz
