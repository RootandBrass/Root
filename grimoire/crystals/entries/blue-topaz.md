geology: |+
  - A blue variety of the mineral **Topaz**, an aluminum fluorosilicate
  - Has a Mohs hardness of **8**, but perfect basal cleavage makes it vulnerable to splitting from sharp impacts
  - Natural blue Topaz is uncommon and usually pale
  - Most commercial Blue Topaz is produced by irradiating and heating colorless Topaz
  - Common trade colors include Sky Blue, Swiss Blue, and London Blue
  - Treatment is generally stable, but careful handling remains important

care: |+
  - Clean with a **soft cloth and mild soapy water**
  - Rinse briefly and dry thoroughly
  - Avoid steam and ultrasonic cleaners
  - Avoid sudden temperature changes, harsh chemicals, and prolonged heat
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Protect from sharp knocks because Topaz can split along cleavage planes
  - Store separately in a padded place
  - Limit prolonged strong sunlight, especially for treated stones
