geology: |-
  - An opaque to finely crystalline variety of **chalcedony**, composed primarily of silicon dioxide
  - Jasper from Mexico occurs in many colors and patterns depending on mineral inclusions and the locality where it formed
  - Iron oxides commonly create red, brown, and yellow coloration, while other mineral inclusions can produce green, cream, or contrasting patterns
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **6.5–7**
  - Individual pieces may show brecciation, banding, spots, orbicular patterns, or landscape-like markings

  - Connected especially with the **root chakra**, with warmer-colored pieces also associated with the sacral chakra
  - Often used as a steadying stone during periods of change or demanding work
  - Its earthy colors and patterns are symbolically associated with resilience, landscape, ancestry, and place

care: |-
  - Clean gently with a **soft, damp cloth** or rinse briefly with lukewarm water
  - Dry thoroughly after cleaning
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid harsh chemicals and sudden temperature changes
  - Although relatively durable, protect polished pieces from hard impacts and abrasive surfaces
  - Store separately from softer stones because jasper can scratch them
