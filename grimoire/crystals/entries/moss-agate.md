geology: |+
  - A translucent to opaque variety of **chalcedony**, the microcrystalline form of quartz
  - Contains branching green, brown, or black mineral inclusions that resemble moss, trees, or landscapes
  - Despite its name, it contains no actual moss or plant material
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **6.5 to 7**
  - Often lacks the regular banding seen in many other agates

associations: |+
  - Traditionally associated with **growth, abundance, nature, patience, renewal, and grounded stability**
  - Connected with the **heart and root chakras**
  - Often called a gardener's stone
  - Associated with gradual progress and recovery after difficult periods

uses: |+
  - Encourages growth and new beginnings
  - Traditionally used for abundance and prosperity
  - Supports gardening, plant magic, and connection with nature
  - Encourages grounding, emotional balance, and patience
  - Supports personal growth and persistence
  - Often carried as a gardener's stone
  - Lovely for garden altars and seasonal rituals

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft, damp cloth**
  - Briefly rinse with cool or lukewarm water and dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid prolonged soaking, saltwater, harsh chemicals, steam, and sudden temperature changes
  - Protect fractures and natural cavities from impacts and trapped moisture
  - Store separately from harder stones

pairings: Flower Agate, Green Aventurine, Jade, Clear Quartz, Red Jasper
