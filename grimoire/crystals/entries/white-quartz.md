title: White Quartz
variety: Milky Quartz
color: White, Cloudy & Translucent
element: Air & Earth
chakra: Crown
zodiac: All Zodiac Signs
geology: |+
  - A white to cloudy variety of **quartz**, composed primarily of silicon dioxide
  - Commonly called **Milky Quartz**
  - Its cloudy appearance is usually caused by microscopic fluid or gas inclusions formed during crystal growth
  - May range from nearly opaque white to translucent with misty veils
  - Has a Mohs hardness of **7**
  - Natural pieces may contain fractures, rainbows, mineral inclusions, or areas of clearer quartz

associations: |+
  - Traditionally associated with **clarity, purification, balance, and gentle amplification**
  - Connected with the **crown chakra**
  - Often regarded as a softer, calmer counterpart to clear quartz
  - Associated with cleansing spaces and bringing scattered energy into balance
  - Sometimes used as an all-purpose stone when a more specialized crystal is unavailable

uses: |+
  - Encourages mental clarity and calm concentration
  - Traditionally used to cleanse and balance energy
  - Supports meditation, reflection, and spiritual awareness
  - Can amplify intentions and the qualities of nearby stones
  - Useful for cleansing altars, rooms, and ritual tools
  - Encourages patience when thoughts or emotions feel clouded
  - Can be carried as a simple all-purpose support stone
  - Lovely for moon work, intention-setting, and quiet contemplation
  - Supports fresh starts and the clearing of stagnant habits

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft, damp cloth**
  - Briefly rinse with cool or lukewarm water, then dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid sudden temperature changes, which may worsen internal fractures
  - Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive cleaners
  - Prolonged strong sunlight may affect included material or create heat stress
  - Store separately from softer stones because quartz can scratch them
  - Protect points and fractured areas from hard impacts

pairings: Clear Quartz, Amethyst, Rose Quartz, Selenite, Labradorite
