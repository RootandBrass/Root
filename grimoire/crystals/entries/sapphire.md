geology: |+
  - A gem variety of **corundum**, composed primarily of aluminum oxide
  - Sapphire occurs in many colors, but the unqualified name commonly refers to blue Sapphire
  - Blue color is typically associated with trace amounts of iron and titanium
  - Has a Mohs hardness of **9**, making it highly resistant to scratching
  - Natural stones commonly contain inclusions and color zoning
  - Heat treatment is common; some stones may also be diffusion-treated or fracture-filled

  - Connected with the **throat and third-eye chakras**
  - Often regarded as a stone of integrity and responsible leadership
  - Associated with learning, devotion, and keeping promises
  - Considered the traditional birthstone for September

care: |+
  - Clean with a **soft cloth and mild soapy water**
  - Rinse and dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid harsh chemicals and sudden temperature changes
  - Protect from hard blows despite Sapphire's excellent hardness
  - Avoid steam or ultrasonic cleaning for fracture-filled, heavily included, or unknown treated stones
  - Store separately because Sapphire can scratch most other gemstones
  - Obtain treatment information before using professional cleaning methods
