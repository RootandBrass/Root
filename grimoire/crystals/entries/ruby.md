geology: |+
  - The red gem variety of **corundum**, composed primarily of aluminum oxide
  - Its red color is produced mainly by trace amounts of chromium
  - Has a Mohs hardness of **9**, second only to diamond among common natural gemstones
  - May fluoresce red under ultraviolet light
  - Natural Ruby commonly contains inclusions; perfectly clear material is rare
  - Heat treatment is common, and some stones may be fracture-filled or otherwise enhanced

associations: |+

  - Connected with the **root and heart chakras**
  - Often regarded as a stone of nobility, confidence, and wholehearted commitment
  - Associated with love expressed through courageous action
  - Considered the traditional birthstone for July

uses: |+
  - Encourages courage, vitality, and confident action

  - Traditionally carried for protection, prosperity, and success

  - Supports Leo warmth and Sagittarius enthusiasm
  - Can be carried during demanding projects or important decisions
  - Encourages acting with confidence while honoring the heart
  - Lovely for an altar centered on strength, devotion, or personal power

care: |+
  - Clean with a **soft cloth and mild soapy water**
  - Rinse and dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid harsh chemicals and sudden temperature changes
  - Protect from hard blows despite Ruby's excellent hardness
  - Do not use steam or ultrasonic cleaning on fracture-filled, heavily included, or unknown treated stones
  - Store separately because Ruby can scratch most other gemstones
  - Obtain treatment information before using professional cleaning methods
