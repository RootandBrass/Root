geology: |+
  - A transparent to translucent variety of **quartz**, composed primarily of silicon dioxide
  - Clear, well-formed specimens are also known as **Rock Crystal**
  - Has a Mohs hardness of **7**
  - Commonly forms six-sided prismatic crystals ending in pointed terminations
  - Natural pieces may contain veils, rainbows, mineral inclusions, phantom growth, or internal fractures
  - Quartz is piezoelectric, producing an electrical charge when subjected to mechanical pressure

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft, damp cloth**
  - Briefly rinse with cool or lukewarm water, then dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid sudden temperature changes, which may worsen internal fractures
  - Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive cleaners, and prolonged soaking
  - Protect points and clusters from knocks or falls
  - Store carefully because quartz can scratch softer stones
  - Keep crystal spheres away from direct sunlight where they could concentrate light and create a fire hazard
