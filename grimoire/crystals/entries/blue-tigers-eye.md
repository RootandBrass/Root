geology: |+
  - A naturally blue-gray, chatoyant variety of quartz commonly called **Hawk's Eye**
  - Forms when quartz replaces fibrous crocidolite while preserving its aligned structure
  - Its silky moving band of light is known as **chatoyancy**
  - Blue Tiger's Eye is generally considered the less-oxidized precursor to golden Tiger's Eye
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **7**, though fractures can still chip
