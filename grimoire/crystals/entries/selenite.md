---
title: Selenite
variety: Selenite
color: White, Clear & Pearly
element: Air
chakra: Crown, Third Eye
zodiac: Taurus, Cancer
geology: |+
  - A transparent to translucent crystalline variety of **gypsum**, composed of hydrated calcium sulfate
  - Often forms as clear blades, fibrous wands, tabular crystals, rosettes, or satin-like masses
  - Many white fibrous pieces sold as selenite are more precisely called **Satin Spar gypsum**
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **2**, so it can be scratched by a fingernail
  - Naturally splits along cleavage planes and can flake, splinter, or chip easily
  - Its name comes from Selene, the ancient Greek personification of the Moon

associations: |+
  - Traditionally associated with **cleansing, clarity, peace, purification, and spiritual awareness**
  - Connected with the **crown and third-eye chakras**
  - Often used to cleanse rooms, ritual tools, and other crystals
  - Associated with moonlight, quiet reflection, and a calm atmosphere
  - Frequently kept on altars, windowsills, or near entrances as a symbol of purification

uses: |+
  - Traditionally used to cleanse and refresh the energy of other stones
  - Encourages calm, clarity, and mental stillness
  - Supports meditation, divination, and spiritual reflection
  - Useful for cleansing altars, rooms, and ritual tools
  - Can be placed near a crystal collection as a symbolic cleansing stone
  - Encourages peaceful sleep and a quieter evening atmosphere
  - Lovely for moon rituals and intention-setting
  - Can be used as a boundary marker around a sacred or restful space
  - Supports releasing mental clutter before journaling or meditation

care: |+
  - **Do not soak or submerge in water.** Selenite is water-sensitive and may dissolve, pit, dull, or weaken
  - Do not cleanse with saltwater or place directly in damp soil
  - Clean only with a **soft, dry cloth or gentle dry brush**
  - Avoid household cleaners, oils, perfumes, and abrasive materials
  - Cleanse energetically with **sound, smoke, visualization, or moonlight**
  - Avoid prolonged exposure to humidity
  - Protect from scratches, pressure, knocks, and falls because selenite is extremely soft
  - Handle fibrous Satin Spar carefully because damaged edges may splinter
  - Store separately from harder stones in a dry place

pairings: Clear Quartz, Amethyst, Moonstone, Rose Quartz, Labradorite
notes: |+
  White fibrous wands and towers commonly sold as selenite are often Satin Spar, which is another variety of gypsum. Both require the same gentle, water-free care.
