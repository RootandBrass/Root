geology: |+
  - A blue-gray chatoyant variety of quartz also known as **Hawk's Eye**
  - Forms when quartz replaces fibrous crocidolite while preserving its aligned structure
  - Its moving silky band of light is called **chatoyancy**
  - Blue Tiger's Eye is generally the less-oxidized precursor to golden Tiger's Eye
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **7**, though fractures may chip

associations: |+
  - Traditionally associated with **calm focus, perception, protection, courage, and clear communication**
  - Connected with the **throat and third-eye chakras**
  - Often used to balance alertness with emotional steadiness
  - Associated with observation and seeing a broader perspective

uses: |+
  - Encourages calm and emotional balance
  - Helps quiet an overactive or scattered mind
  - Supports clear communication and speaking one's truth
  - Enhances intuition, perception, and mental clarity
  - Traditionally used for protection
  - Useful for meditation and divination
  - Encourages courage without overly fiery energy
  - Supports grounding and spiritual awareness

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft, damp cloth**
  - Briefly rinse with lukewarm water and dry thoroughly
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid prolonged soaking, saltwater, harsh chemicals, steam, and sudden temperature changes
  - Protect fractures from hard impacts
  - Store separately from harder stones
  - Limit prolonged strong sunlight
