geology: |+
  - A banded variety of **chalcedony**, the microcrystalline form of quartz
  - Forms as silica-rich layers deposited inside cavities in volcanic or sedimentary rock
  - Its bands record changes in mineral content and conditions during growth
  - The open pocket is lined with sparkling **druzy quartz** crystals
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **6.5 to 7**, though the druzy pocket is vulnerable to chipping

care: |+
  - Clean gently with a **soft, slightly damp cloth**
  - Cleanse energetically with **moonlight, smoke, sound, or selenite**
  - Avoid prolonged soaking, saltwater, and harsh chemicals
  - Do not use steam or ultrasonic cleaners on a specimen with cavities or fractures
  - Keep moisture and debris from collecting in the druzy pocket
  - Protect the exposed crystals from chips and scratches
  - Store separately from harder stones
