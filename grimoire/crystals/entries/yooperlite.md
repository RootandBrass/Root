geology: |+
  - A trade name for **sodalite-bearing syenite** found as glacial cobbles around the Great Lakes
  - Appears gray, brown, black, or speckled in ordinary light
  - Sodalite-bearing areas fluoresce vivid orange or yellow-orange under long-wave ultraviolet light
  - The rock contains several minerals, so hardness and texture vary across a specimen
  - The name was popularized for material collected along Michigan's Upper Peninsula
