  - A naturally smoky brown to gray variety of **quartz**, composed primarily of silicon dioxide
  - Its color forms when natural radiation interacts with trace aluminum within the crystal structure
  - Shades range from pale translucent gray-brown to very dark brown or nearly black specimens
  - Has a Mohs hardness of **7**, making it durable but still vulnerable to chips, fractures, and hard impacts
  - Well-formed crystals may occur as six-sided prisms, points, clusters, or massive material
  - Very dark material is sometimes sold as **Morion**, a traditional name for nearly black smoky quartz
