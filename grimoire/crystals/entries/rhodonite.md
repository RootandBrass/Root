geology: |+
  - A manganese silicate mineral known for its pink to rose-red coloring
  - Frequently contains black manganese-oxide veins, patches, or dendritic markings
  - Has a Mohs hardness of approximately **5.5 to 6.5**
  - Commonly found in massive form and fashioned into tumbled stones, beads, carvings, and cabochons
  - Color may range from soft dusty pink to deep reddish pink
  - Natural fractures and differences in hardness can make some specimens vulnerable to chipping
