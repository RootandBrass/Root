ROOT & BRASS JOURNAL

Welcome to Root & Brass

August 2026

Root & Brass began as a place to gather the things that seem worth remembering.

An old house with more than a century behind it. A garden that changes every season. Projects, discoveries, research, photographs, stories, and the ordinary details that slowly become part of a place.

This journal is where those pieces can live together. Some entries will come from 301 Ruby. Others will come from the garden, from something discovered, researched, restored, planted, or simply noticed.

Root & Brass does not need to become one particular thing. It can grow as the house, garden, and stories around them continue to grow.
